# Subscriptions and Usage

## Plan Limits

Free plans include 3 users, 2 projects, and 500 automation runs per month. Pro plans include 25 users, unlimited projects, and 25,000 automation runs per month. Enterprise limits are defined by contract.

## Storage Limits

Free plans include 1 GB of storage. Pro plans include 100 GB of storage. Enterprise storage is contracted separately and can be expanded by contacting sales.

## Usage Notifications

Workspace admins receive email notifications at 80%, 95%, and 100% of monthly automation usage. When usage reaches 100%, automations pause until the next billing cycle or until the workspace upgrades.

## Seat Billing

Pro plans are billed per active seat. Removing a user stops future seat billing but does not create a prorated refund for the current billing period.
