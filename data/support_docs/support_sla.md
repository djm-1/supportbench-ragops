# Support SLA

## Support Channels

Free customers receive community support. Pro customers receive email support. Enterprise customers receive email and shared Slack channel support when included in their contract.

## Response Times

Pro support targets a first response within one business day. Enterprise priority-one incidents target a first response within one hour. Community support does not have a guaranteed response time.

## Priority Levels

Priority-one incidents include full production outage, data loss risk, or security incident. Priority-two incidents include major feature degradation. Priority-three incidents include questions, minor defects, or configuration help.

## Escalation

Enterprise customers can request escalation through their shared Slack channel or designated customer success manager. Escalations require a business impact summary.
