# Cancellations and Plan Changes

## Cancel Subscription

Workspace owners can cancel a subscription from Settings > Billing > Manage Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. The workspace remains usable until the paid period ends.

## Downgrades

Plan downgrades also take effect at the end of the current billing period. If the workspace exceeds the limits of the lower plan, admins must reduce users, storage, or automation usage before the downgrade can be completed.

## Reactivation

Cancelled workspaces can be reactivated within 30 days without data loss. After 30 days, archived workspace data may be queued for deletion under the data retention policy.

## Enterprise Cancellation

Enterprise contracts follow the cancellation terms in the signed order form. Self-service cancellation is not available for enterprise workspaces.
