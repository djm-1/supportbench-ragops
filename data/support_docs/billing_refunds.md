# Billing and Refunds

## Refund Policy

Annual plans are refundable within 14 days of purchase if workspace usage stays below the fair-use threshold of 100 automation runs and 5 GB of storage. Monthly plans are not refundable after the billing cycle starts, except when a duplicate charge is confirmed by support.

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer sees two charges for the same workspace and billing period, support can reverse the duplicate charge after verifying the payment processor transaction IDs. Duplicate charge reviews usually take three to five business days.

## Failed Payments

When a payment fails, the account remains active for a seven-day grace period. The billing owner receives email notices on day one, day three, and day six. If payment is not updated by the end of the grace period, the workspace becomes read-only.

## Tax Invoices

Tax invoices are generated automatically after successful payment. Billing owners can download invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Tax ID changes only apply to future invoices and cannot be applied retroactively.
