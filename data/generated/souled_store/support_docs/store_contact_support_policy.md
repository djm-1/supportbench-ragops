# The Souled Store Store, Contact, and Support Policy

## Stores and try-before-buy

The Souled Store is present offline as well as online. Customers can check stores near them at https://www.thesouledstore.com/stores-near-me. There is no try-and-buy option for website or app purchases, but customers can use the size chart on product pages to pick the right size. Customers can also visit retail stores where The Souled Store is present in their city.

## Customer support channels

Customers can contact The Souled Store at connect@thesouledstore.com. For delivered-but-not-received issues, customers can also call 022-68493328. Delivery disputes should be reported within 3 days of the delivery date so that the courier dispute can be raised.

Customers can contact support for manufacturing issues, return or exchange concerns, payment issues, and order delivery problems. Manufacturing issue support may ask for product images and proof before confirming the next action.

## Contest and other policy links

The Souled Store may run contests. Standard contest terms and conditions are available at https://www.thesouledstore.com/contest-terms-and-conditions. Customers should refer to that page for contest-specific terms rather than assuming normal order policies apply.
