# The Souled Store Shipping and Tracking Policy

## Delivery timelines and shipping fees

Orders shipped within India usually reach metro locations within 1 to 4 business days after dispatch. Orders for the rest of India usually take 4 to 7 business days after dispatch. These estimates can move because of public holidays, extreme weather, local courier constraints, or other delivery conditions. Pre-order products are shipped on the date shown in the product description as far as possible, although unusual demand or operational issues can cause a delay. When there is a pre-order delay, the customer experience team keeps the customer updated.

Members receive free shipping on all orders. Non-members pay a shipping charge of Rs 50 when the order value is below Rs 499. Non-member orders of Rs 499 or more do not require that shipping charge.

## Tracking and delivery exceptions

Once an order leaves the warehouse, The Souled Store sends tracking information by email, SMS, and WhatsApp. Customers can also track an order from their website account by opening Orders from the top-right account area and selecting Track Order for the relevant order ID.

If a prepaid order cannot be delivered and the courier returns it to The Souled Store, the refund is initiated as TSS Money in the customer's Souled Store account. This TSS Money normally reflects within 24 to 48 hours of initiation.

When tracking says an order has been delivered but the customer has not received it, the customer should contact connect@thesouledstore.com or call 022-68493328. The issue must be reported within 3 days of the delivery date so that The Souled Store can raise a dispute with the courier partner.

## Cancellation and address changes

Customers cannot modify the shipping address after the system has already passed the mobile number and address to the warehouse for packing and shipping. If the order has not been packed yet, the customer can cancel the order and place a new one with the correct address.

There are no cancellation charges. A cancellation is only available before dispatch. After dispatch, the customer must use the return or exchange process where applicable.

## Return status tracking

Customers can track return status from the My Orders page by checking the product for which a return was created. This return tracking path is separate from the order delivery tracking path.
