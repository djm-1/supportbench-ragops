# The Souled Store Returns, Exchange, and Refund Policy

## Return policy and refund timing

Customers can return an order within 30 days after delivery. The Souled Store provides reverse pickup for most pin codes. For the return to be accepted, the product must comply with the return policy and must be picked up by the reverse pickup team. Customers may also return or exchange products at eligible nearest stores.

Refunds are processed after the product is received and checked by the customer experience team. Once confirmed, the refund is provided either into the customer's bank account or as TSS Money according to the customer's convenience. If the return product was bought on discount, the refund is only for the exact amount paid for that product.

Gift cards and vouchers are non-refundable. Gift wrapping charges are not refunded when goods are returned, and The Souled Store does not provide gift wrap replacements. The company may change applicable return or exchange categories at its discretion and without prior notice.

## Exchange policy and reverse pickup

Customers can apply for an exchange within 30 days after an order has been delivered. A reverse pickup facility is available for most pin codes. Exchanges are limited to the same product in a different size. The product cannot be exchanged for another design in the same product category or for a different product on the website or app.

If an exchange request is created, someone from the courier partner's team usually arrives at the address for pickup within 2 business days. The product and tags must remain intact for the courier company to accept the pickup. When the old product is picked up, the exchanged product is delivered within 7 days of the pickup date.

If a customer exchanges products from a combo pack, the whole pack must be sent back. Partial returns from combo packs are not accepted.

## Self-ship returns and hygiene exclusions

If the customer's pin code is not serviceable for reverse pickup, the customer must courier the product to K-Square Logistics Park, Prakhhyat Land Developers Private Ltd Warehouse, Building No. 13A, Near Padgha Toll Naka, On Mumbai-Nashik Highway, Bhiwandi, Maharashtra 4211011. The item must be packed securely to avoid loss or damage during transit, and it must be unused with original tags attached and packaging intact.

After the self-shipped product is received within 24 hours, The Souled Store credits the self-ship courier charge to the customer's TSS Money and gives 100 TSS points as a thank-you for using self-ship. Customers are asked not to use The Professional Couriers for self-return because the package may not be accepted at the warehouse. Speed Post is recommended because it has a wide postal network.

For hygiene reasons, returns and exchanges may be refused where the item has been worn, washed, soiled, defective due to customer handling, or sent without tags. The Souled Store also does not accept returns or exchanges for several hygiene-sensitive product categories, including masks, boxers, shorts, sweat-activated t-shirts, and socks.

## Exceptional exchange and sale policies

Some exchanges may be allowed only in exceptional cases and at the sole discretion of The Souled Store. These exceptional requests must be made within 48 hours of purchase by contacting customer support. The product must be unused, in original condition, with tags intact and proof of purchase. If approved, the exchange is subject to stock availability and may be processed for size replacement or store credit, not a different product.

Purchases made during festival sales, promotional events, or pop-up stalls can be strictly non-refundable. Prices during festival sales may differ from prices on the website or retail stores because of exclusive promotions, and no price adjustment is made for differences across sales channels.
