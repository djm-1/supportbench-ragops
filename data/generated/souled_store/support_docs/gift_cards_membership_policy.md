# The Souled Store Gift Card and Membership Policy

## Gift card redemption and validity

The Souled Store gift card has a scratch code at the bottom right. Scratching the card reveals a 16 digit code. The code can be used either at retail stores or online. Online customers enter the code in the Gift Voucher section on the cart page and apply it. Retail customers can ask store staff to use the gift code during billing.

Gift cards can be redeemed against one order only. Gift cards are valid until October 2026. Gift cards and vouchers are non-refundable.

## Gift card refunds

When a customer returns a product purchased with a gift card, the order amount up to Rs 2000, which is the gift card value, is credited back to the gift card. Customers should retain gift cards in case they return an order so that the card can be reused after the return has been processed.

If the order amount was higher than the gift card value, the balance amount above the gift card value is credited back to the original purchase method or to TSS Money according to the customer's choice.

## Membership benefits and cancellation

Membership gives customers free shipping on all orders. Non-members pay Rs 50 shipping on orders below Rs 499. Membership cancellation does not create a mid-way refund. Once membership is purchased, it cannot be cancelled for a proportional refund during the membership term.
