# The Souled Store Orders and Payment Policy

## Placing orders and payment failures

Customers place orders by selecting products, choosing the right size and quantity, adding items to the cart, and completing checkout. If a payment fails, the customer should check whether money was deducted from the payment method. If money was deducted but the order was not created, the amount is normally reversed by the payment provider. The customer can contact support if the reversal does not happen within the expected banking window.

The Souled Store can send order-related communication by email, SMS, and WhatsApp. Customers should keep their contact details accurate so delivery and tracking communication reaches them.

## TSS Money, discounts, and sale pricing

TSS Money is store credit that can be used on eligible purchases through the website or app. Some refunds, including refunds for undelivered prepaid orders, can be credited as TSS Money. Refunds for approved self-ship courier charges are also credited as TSS Money.

When a product bought at a discount is returned, the customer receives only the exact amount paid for the product. Prices during festival sales or promotional events can differ from regular website or retail store prices, and The Souled Store does not adjust prices across channels after purchase.

## Marketing communication and account preferences

After registration, customers may automatically get subscribed to marketing emails and SMS messages. Customers who do not want these messages can use the unsubscribe link in the email.
