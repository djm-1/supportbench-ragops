# The Souled Store About Us

## Brand profile

The Souled Store is an Indian pop-culture and lifestyle brand. Its public positioning focuses on licensed merchandise, original designs, and products across apparel, accessories, and everyday lifestyle categories. The brand operates online and also has offline retail stores.

## Product range

The product range includes items such as t-shirts, oversized t-shirts, shirts, dresses, hoodies, joggers, shorts, boxers, socks, sneakers, bags, phone covers, mugs, badges, and other fandom or lifestyle merchandise.
